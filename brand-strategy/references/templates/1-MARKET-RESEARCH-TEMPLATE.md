> 📋 **Purpose:** The evidence base every downstream document stands on. This report establishes three things: (1) why the incumbent framework/category fails, (2) who the competitors are and what each one misses, (3) where the market is moving — converging on **the strategic gap** the brand will claim.
>
> 📋 **Write this first.** Research precedes strategy: no positioning, naming, or visual decision is made before this document exists. It feeds the Brand Strategy (doc 2) the category opportunity, the Positioning (doc 3) its competitive map and repositioning ammunition, and the PR Narrative (doc 5) its citable science/data arsenal.
>
> 📋 **Rules of evidence:**
> - Every claim gets a number and a source. "Users are dissatisfied" is an opinion; "accuracy ranges 50–82% in RD testing (source)" is evidence.
> - Research what the evidence says, not what the strategy wants. If the data undermines the intended posture, that is the most valuable finding in the report — surface it, don't bury it.
> - Be fair to competitors. Credit what each genuinely does well; the gap is only real if it survives an honest reading of the landscape.
> - Distinguish claim types (e.g., a company's claimed accuracy vs. independent testing; identification accuracy vs. estimation accuracy). Conflating them produces strategy built on sand.
>
